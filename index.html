A SIMPLE WAY TO PROGRESS

Make every set count.

01 Keep a little in reserve

Finish most working sets with 1–3 good reps left. Warm-up sets come before the listed working sets.

02 Earn the next weight

Reach the top of the rep range on every set with good technique and intended effort, then add the smallest practical load.

03 Recover between efforts

Rest 2–3 minutes for compounds, 60–90 seconds for isolation. Take longer when needed.

Build your understanding.

A set is a group of repetitions; 3 × 10 means three sets of ten reps. The guides show working sets, after your warm-up. “Reps in reserve” means how many more controlled repetitions you could perform. Pick exercises you can do with control and adjust the suggestions to your experience.

Choose a plan that fits you.

The upper-body focus preset highlights upper chest, back, and side/rear delts. It is optional, not an assessment of your body. Keep lower-body training in your routine too. The five-day plan is an example rather than a requirement for beginners; adjust training to your experience and recovery.

A little help getting started

  1. Choose a muscle. Hover for a preview, then click or tap to keep it selected. The list and dropdown work too.
  2. Find a movement. Filter by available equipment. Primary-target exercises appear first; supporting movements are labeled.
  3. Learn the technique. Open a movement guide or a related YouTube video. Suggestions are starting points, not instructions to do every exercise.
  4. Build your session. Tap +, open My workout, and choose the date. Log weights, reps, and completed sets.
  5. Come back to your progress. Use the same browser and back up your records regularly. Bookmark the site for quick access at the gym.

Your data & our sources

Workout records stay in your browser

This app saves your sets and notes in browser storage. We do not receive those records. There is no account or cross-device sync. Clearing site data can erase them; use My workout → Backup / restore to keep a copy.

External services

Netlify hosts this public website and processes requests to serve it. YouTube links open external pages only when selected; we do not embed video players or load their thumbnails. Those services have their own privacy policies. No audience analytics or marketing forms have been added.

Exercise information

Technique guides use general exercise principles, with references to NASM and published resistance-training research. Video links belong to their creators; this site is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, NASM, or the featured channels. Popularity does not prove technique quality.

The muscle diagram is simplified. Sets and rep ranges are adjustable examples, not individualized coaching. Choose movements suited to your experience and ask a qualified trainer when unsure.
